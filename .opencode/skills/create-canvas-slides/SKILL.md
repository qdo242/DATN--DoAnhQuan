# Skill: Create Canvas/HTML Slides

Khi user yeu cau tao slide, tao file HTML su dung reveal.js. Luu vao `doandocs/slides.html`, mo trong browser.

## Quy tac

- Mot y chinh moi slide
- toi da 5 bullet points/slide
- Bang toi da 6 cot
- Dung hinh anh thay van ban khi co the
- Slide title cho moi chuong
- Slide cuoi: "Cam on"

## Cau truc HTML

- Dung reveal.js CDN: `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/reveal.js@4.6.0/dist/reveal.min.css`
- Theme: white
- Mau chinh: #003366 (xanh dam)
- Slide number: bat
- Hash: true (de share link)

## Luu tru

- File: `doandocs/slides.html`
- Mo: `start doandocs/slides.html`

## Navigation

- Space/Arrow: slide tiep/truoc
- Esc: overview mode
- F: fullscreen
