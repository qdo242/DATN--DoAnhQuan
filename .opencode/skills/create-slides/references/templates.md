## Styling Guide

- **Background**: White
- **Title Color**: Blue (#003366)
- **Body Color**: Black (#000000)
- **Font**: Calibri
- **Title Size**: 36pt
- **Body Size**: 18pt
- **Slide Size**: 13.333 x 7.5 inches (widescreen)

## Thesis Structure

1. Title page
2. Table of contents
3. Chapter 1: Introduction (3 sections)
4. Chapter 2: Literature (4 sections)
5. Chapter 3: Design (4 sections)
6. Chapter 4: Implementation (7 sections)
7. Chapter 5: Testing (3 sections)
8. Conclusion
9. Q&A
